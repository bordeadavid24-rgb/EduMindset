## Educational mechanism
A read/hide/recall/reveal exercise demonstrates active recall. It has no timer, score, diagnosis, or mandatory input. The method is explained in plain Romanian. Native controls and a static no-JavaScript explanation remain available.

## Image treatment
Hero: background extraction through the built-in image tool, retaining the supplied person and microscope, then responsive alpha AVIF/WebP derivatives. This is an AI-assisted edit of the supplied photo, not a new photo of a different person. The result must be inspected against the source before shipping. Portrait: full photo integrated with a first-person note. Auditorium: art-directed crop integrated with the UnitED dialogue composition, without implying an existing UnitED event.
