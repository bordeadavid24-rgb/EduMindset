## ceiling

The composition now has a recognisable educational voice and sufficient variation: photographic hero, participatory exercise, ruled programme, saturated founder region, open editorial text, and UnitED image/caption pairing. At 1920px the capped reading area stays coherent. At 820px the founder crop is close but retains the head and balances the adjacent note. At 320px some headlines naturally take more lines without collisions. Dark mode preserves hierarchy and the exercise's identity.

This clears the visual finish bar for user review. Further cosmetic iteration without user feedback has diminishing value. The remaining ceilings are actual user preference, real-device feel, and the reported mobile main-thread cost, rather than an evidenced layout failure. Screenshots cannot establish hover, focus, motion, or mail delivery; source supports the stated behaviour, and separate DOM checks are reported, not independently rerun here.
