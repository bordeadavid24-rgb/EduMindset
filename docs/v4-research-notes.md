## Four compositional moves

1. **Hero as a notebook spread with a photographic subject.** Keep the large Alex/microscope cutout as the main asset. Put a slightly imperfect paper contour behind it, a modest charcoal offset plane, and one red pen annotation connected to a real part of the scene. Preserve the existing proposition and welcome as readable HTML. A red underline can double as the seam joining title and scene. Avoid a giant loose field of arbitrary symbols.
2. **A margin drawing with a job.** Create an original small microscope or open-notebook diagram in native SVG, placed in an outer margin rather than beside every headline. Pair it with an arrow that points toward the actual “Încearcă o metodă” action or learning demonstration. The sketch explains exploration; it should not impersonate Alex's handwriting or add a new claim. Reuse one line grammar in the resources and contact regions.
3. **The exercise as a three-panel comic sequence.** “Citește”, “Amintește-ți”, and “Verifică” can be persistent HTML stage labels, with only the current panel active and the existing read/hide/reveal behavior preserved. A small original closed-notebook drawing can accompany the recall stage. The transformation makes the method visible instead of merely decorating its container. Keep one main control per state and no timer, score, or forced progress animation.
4. **Founder and UnitED as different paper compositions.** The founder letter gets an inset real portrait with an irregular but restrained paper backing, while its prose remains on a straight reading baseline. UnitED can use the auditorium photo as a wider print with a small offset caption strip and a single speech-outline motif. These should feel like different pages of the same publication, not the same rotated photo-card repeated. Do not make the speech outline imply a quote Alex never supplied.

## Original SVG drawing system

- Use a small authored set: microscope, notebook/open-to-closed pages, connected question/answer lines, one bracket, one directional arrow, and one underline. Draw the paths specifically for this site; do not trace or download artwork from the references.
- Line work: roughly 2 to 3px at intended display size, round caps and joins, slightly asymmetric paths, occasional incomplete second stroke. Use charcoal for structure and brand red for a selected emphasis. Avoid a uniform icon-library look and avoid adding jitter through animation.
- Keep a drawing readable as a silhouette and limit interior detail. Use one modest hand-drawn correction mark or double underline where it highlights an actual word. No random equations, fake teacher feedback, fabricated handwriting, stars, smiley mascots, alphabet blocks, or kindergarten clipart.
- A cut-paper backing can use a short original polygon or SVG contour. Keep its torn edge away from body text. One or two displaced layers are sufficient; use little or no shadow, and keep the source photograph in natural colour.
- Decorative SVGs should have `aria-hidden="true"`, no keyboard focus, and `pointer-events: none`. Informative steps and labels stay real text. A diagram that conveys unique information needs an accessible text explanation.
- Do not place important words inside an SVG, imitate a signature, or use private character descriptions as annotation copy.

## Mobile and performance

Use a linear page at narrow widths. Reduce, reposition, or hide margin art instead of scaling an entire desktop collage into unreadable miniatures. Keep the face, gloved hand, microscope, CTA, and 16px reading copy clear at 320px. Maintain at least 44px controls. A 1 to 2 degree tilt can be reserved for a paper backing; body copy and inputs stay upright.

The comic exercise should stack or show one active panel within stable geometry, without requiring horizontal scrolling or hover. Text and control order must remain coherent without JavaScript. Reduced motion keeps state changes immediate; no bobbing stickers, parallax paper, or perpetual line drawing.

Prefer compact inline SVG and existing responsive images. No canvas loop, heavy drawing library, animated turbulence filter, grain overlay, or new texture download is needed. Preserve hero dimensions and format-specific preload, lazy-load lower photography, and reserve any collage backing space so its load does not move text. Keep the red plane/art outside hit areas.

## Verification target

The result should visibly contain original authored drawing and collage composition while the programme, founder letter, and actions remain easier to understand than the artwork. Check one desktop/mobile batch for clipped marks, hidden controls, contrast, exercise states, and image crops, then fix observed defects together. Do not claim that decorative notebook imagery itself improves learning.
