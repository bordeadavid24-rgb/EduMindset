## Design interpretation — recommendations, not verified brand rules

**Direction: an open workshop with a real teacher.** The defining combination is scientific curiosity plus human encouragement. Students should feel invited to think and try something, while parents can immediately identify the accountable person behind it. The site should have the energy of a conversation already beginning.

1. Let the supplied sentence “Nu este suficient să înveți. Contează cum înveți.” carry the hero. A single highlighted “cum” connects typography with the project's actual proposition. Pair it with a real photograph from the supplied assets, rather than a generic brain render, graduation cap or decorative laptop mockup.
2. Introduce Alexandru early, with “Salut!” and his actual name visible. Give his photo a generous, straightforward frame and set the introduction as a personal note. Keep the three supplied credentials readable in one line or compact paragraph; avoid badge walls, invented affiliations, years-of-experience counters or academic seals.
3. Use a **question → exploration → direction** thread. A small marginal question, a purposeful underline and a connecting line can recur sparingly. These mean something for this founder. Avoid making every section a graph-paper canvas or scattering childish school doodles everywhere.
4. Compose varied sections: a bold opening statement, a photo and personal note, three unboxed principles, a workshop agenda, a distinct UnitED announcement, then useful resources and contact. Rhythmic changes should come from content and photographs, not arbitrary background changes and endless repeated cards.
5. Prefer clear sans-serif typography with a voice, comfortable Romanian body copy and proper diacritics. A handwritten accent can work as one short annotation if the supplied brand imagery supports it. It must not become the body type or an invented signature.
6. Take the palette from verified supplied logo/photography. If the assets do not establish one, choose a legible white/light neutral ground and one confident accent; make that decision explicit. Do not infer beige, brass or luxury styling from “personality.”

## A small interaction that earns its place

An optional workshop selector can ask “De unde vrei să începi?” with the four supplied areas: learning, time, confidence, future. Selecting one exposes its actual workshop topics in a stable panel. This models active participation and helps a visitor find relevance. It must work by keyboard and touch, show an obvious selected state, and remain readable without motion. No personality diagnosis, score, mandatory quiz or claim to identify a person's learning style.

Use the workshop notice honestly: “Workshop-urile urmează!” plus Instagram and contact actions. The QR must encode the real Instagram URL and have a conventional link alongside it. No countdown, ticket purchase or “reserve your place” button while date/registration are unknown. UnitED and resource items need clear forthcoming status rather than clickable empty content.

## Emil Kowalski skill application

Read `/Users/lilianab/.agents/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md`. For this site, being alive should mean responsive feedback and readable state changes:

- Buttons respond on press with scale around 0.97–0.98, 100–160 ms.
- Workshop switching can crossfade/translate a few pixels over 150–220 ms using a strong ease-out. Keep panel space stable; never animate from scale zero.
- A one-time, modest photo/text entrance can establish the story, but content must be visible if scripting or observation fails.
- Use transform and opacity. Respect reduced motion. Keep keyboard actions immediate.
- Avoid scroll hijacking, mouse-following cursors, perpetual spinning stickers, fake parallax and text hidden behind long reveal sequences. These compete with the open, attentive mentor personality.
