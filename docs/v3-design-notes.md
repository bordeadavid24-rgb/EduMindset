# V3: an invitation into Alex's teaching studio

Research recommendation, 10 November 2026. Reviewed the current V2 HTML/CSS, product and direction records, final desktop/mobile opening captures, and the Emil Kowalski, design-taste, and Impeccable guidance. No app files edited.

Reading this as: a personal Romanian educational invitation, grounded in the real founder and his teaching practice, with the original red and black identity. Richness should come from photographic composition, an individual voice, and useful detail rather than ornaments or luxury styling.

## Content boundary

Private feedback guides design decisions only. Do not publish personality descriptions, inferred motives, praise, invented endorsements, or new biographical claims. Use the supplied first-person introduction and verified qualifications. “Salut! Eu sunt Alexandru-Constantin Tulică.” and “Am creat EDU MindSet din convingerea...” are already available; they can make the page personal without adding a character assessment.

## Specific composition choices

### Hero: photo first, shared space

Use the source-derived cutout already built, preserving Alex, the gloved hand, microscope, and table base. Keep the person visually larger than the graphic accent. The red field is a simple plane behind the apparatus, not a halo, sticker, circle, or stage light. A thin dark baseline can connect the actual table to the page edge; do not add a fake physical shadow or invented laboratory scenery.

The main copy can return to the user's “Nu este suficient să înveți. Contează cum înveți.” Avoid competing new slogans. A short supplied first-person passage gives authorship near the main action. Place the workshop CTA and an optional method link with that passage, not as detached badges over the image. Do not overlay text on the face or apparatus.

Aim for deliberate differences in scale: one very large photographic subject, one strong proposition, one quiet personal paragraph. Do not fill remaining space with little labels. The laboratory scene is the hero proof; save the other photographs for their own meaningful moments rather than loading all three in the opening.

### Founder: an open letter, not a tribute

Use the formal photograph with eye contact. Preserve its colour and a little real setting, but avoid extending the library's wood/brass atmosphere into the website. A square or modest portrait crop at roughly one-third of the desktop region is enough. The letter begins at the same horizontal baseline as the image, then continues into a broad open reading area rather than being trapped in a card.

The supplied line “Cred în educația care oferă încredere, deschide perspective...” can be the enlarged passage. Keep it short and attributed to the person already speaking. His three qualifications belong together in ordinary text, without trophy icons, counters, superlatives, or a credentials wall. Long background can remain in a native disclosure.

### Programme: a teaching sequence

Let the Romanian topic names lead, as V2 already does. Make Learning Skills and its concrete recall demonstration the first working area. The other directions remain easy to open and compare. Integrate the upcoming announcement directly at the end of the programme so the visitor sees why the next action is Instagram rather than a booking form.

Use the auditorium photograph later inside UnitED as a wide contextual image, paired with the actual upcoming status and supplied dialogue-oriented copy. Keep enough of the setting to make it feel like a real moment. Do not state or imply that it documents an earlier UnitED event.

## Red and black system

Restore the original black EDU and vertical red MindSet relationship. Use a near-white base, near-black reading text, and one clear brand red, supported by pale red only where a learning region needs separation. Suggested implementation starting points: white #FAFAF8, ink #191919, accent #D6423A, accessible action red #B62F2A, soft note #F6EAE8. Sample the supplied logo to refine the exact accent. Red buttons must be checked with white text; visual similarity is not a contrast check.

Remove cobalt, yellow, and orange from the interface rather than allowing two identities to compete. Keep the original photographs in colour. Do not add monochrome filters merely to make the page look serious. One charcoal programme region may provide a strong change of rhythm if its controls, captions, and exercise states remain legible; the page does not need alternating dark sections.

Retain one local variable family unless the actual implementation demonstrates a reading problem. Strong type hierarchy can come from weight, optical sizing, and scale. Do not add a luxury serif, cursive signature, or generic handwritten annotations to manufacture personality.

## Mobile and performance risks

- The two-row hero must be recomposed below 768px, not absolutely positioned as a shrunken desktop. Use proposition, short welcome, primary action, then the complete photo scene. Reserve the image's space and keep the face and microscope visible at 320px.
- On small screens, remove decorative overlaps and reduce the red field to a modest grounding strip. Do not put body text on the cutout or make a huge image push every useful action below the opening.
- The founder letter should begin with greeting/name and a compact portrait, then flow into readable 16px text. A full-screen formal headshot before any introduction will feel distant and delay the content.
- Keep the recall interaction optional and preserve its read/recall/reveal/reset behavior. Explain reamintire activă beside the exercise. Do not require a timer, grade, or personal information. A control inside a disclosure must remain reachable and understandable by keyboard.
- Preserve the existing responsive AVIF/WebP derivatives, mobile WebP choice, explicit dimensions, and matching hero preload. Only the main hero image should receive image preload priority. Lazy-load the portrait and auditorium image.
- Keep 44px hit areas, visible focus, 12px minimum small hero/brand text, 16px form inputs, safe-area spacing, reduced motion, and native disclosure fallbacks. Test the new programme at 320px and tablet widths, where split structures can become cramped.
- Do not reintroduce content-visibility estimates or heavy scroll effects. The earlier optimisation was removed because full-page capture geometry was unstable. CSS layout and small native interactions are sufficient.

## What to verify

One bounded desktop/mobile inspection should show a materially different hero topology, an authored founder letter, and a programme that connects the demonstration to a real topic. Check head/hand/apparatus crops, both colour themes if retained, contrast, recall state heights, and deep links opening workshops. Inspect the original and cutout side by side if the image is changed again. No claim of final testing is made by this research note.
