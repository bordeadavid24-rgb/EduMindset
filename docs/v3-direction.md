# V3: a personal teaching space

Latest user direction: Alex finds V2 too simplistic. Keep the original logo's red/black colourway, make the design more personal and developed, and avoid a generic luxury aesthetic. The user's personal description of Alex is design context only. Do not publish it as copy, praise, testimonials, metadata or claims about his character.

## Design read
Reading this as a teacher's own invitation: real photographs, original first-person words, and a working programme that gives visitors something to try. Design variance 7, motion intensity 4, density 5. The open learning-studio world evolves through more specific composition and authorship, not through invented biographical facts.

## Changes that carry the brief
- Original brand red and charcoal on a clear near-white base. Brighter logo red is used as artwork; darker red keeps small controls legible. Dark mode remains neutral/brand red.
- A two-row, twelve-column opening: Alex's welcome, his actual supplied motivation and a larger photographic scene cross a restrained red plane. No giant question mark or unrelated palette.
- A personal, unboxed open letter with an eye-contact portrait inset and his original account of founding the project. Qualifications are factual, quiet and secondary.
- A programme that feels worked through: the optional active-recall activity belongs to Learning Skills, alongside the complete original topics. Other topics remain easy to scan/open.
- Documentary photos and typographic notes change scale across the page. No fake signatures, classroom artifacts, testimonials, awards, school affiliations or handwritten claims.

## Preserve
Romanian source copy and diacritics, all six required areas, workshop/UnitED/resource forthcoming status, honest mailto delivery, keyboard/reduced-motion support, responsive images and the untouched MINA render. Keep no em dashes in authored page copy. Explain new techniques in the guide and maintain HANDOFF/GROK_PROMPT.

## Mobile
Reflow into a readable welcome, visible action, whole face/instrument and then letter/programme. Overlaps are controlled by the grid, not fixed heights over text. No hover-only information; no large new libraries or new generated photographs.
